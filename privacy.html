Privacy Policy

60 Days Of Zombies · Last updated: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the 60 Days Of Zombies mobile game (the “App”), provided by Youssef (“we”, “us”), handles information. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, or other personal details ourselves. The App does show advertisements, and the advertising service we use collects some data, as described below.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information directly. The App does not request your contacts, photos, microphone, or precise location.

2. Information stored on your device

Your game progress, settings, and scores are saved locally on your device. We do not receive this data. It is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its storage.

3. Advertising

The App displays ads provided by Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service. To show and measure ads, AdMob may automatically collect and use information such as:

  • your device’s advertising ID;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device information (model, operating system, language);
  • how you interact with ads and how the App performs.

This information may be used to show you personalized or non-personalized ads, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance. We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you. Learn how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

4. Your choices

  • On Android, you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in Settings ← Google ← Ads.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you may be asked for consent to personalized ads, and you can change your choice at any time where the App provides that option.

5. Google Play

You download the App through Google Play. Google may collect information about your installation and use of Google Play under its own privacy policy, which we do not control.

6. Children

The App is intended for users aged 13 and over, and it contains zombie-themed horror content. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security and retention

We do not store your personal information on our servers. Data collected by advertising partners is held under their own retention policies.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change how the App handles information, we will update this policy and the “Last updated” date above.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Write to us at 60daysofzomies@gmail.com.
